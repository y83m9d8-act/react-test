Know the signs: How to tell if your cat/dog has stiff joints ?

Stiff joints can have serious effects on your pet’s overall quality of life. Keep an eye open for the warning signs to look out for.

Does your cat/dog…
•    show stiffness, especially after resting?
•    hesitate to go up and down stairs?
•    lag behind or tire easily during walks?
•    prefer to lie down rather than sit or stand?
•    whimper, growl or snap when you touch his joints?

If you have answered yes to some of the above, then your pet may be suffering from stiff joints. Please make sure you consult your vet.

 Hills JD is clinically proven to improve mobility and help your pet walk, run and jump better in as little as 21 days (28 days for cats). Hills JD has the right balance of healthy nutrients and special omega-3 oil.
